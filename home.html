ABOUT US

KAPB: Preserving Heritage, Embracing Opportunities Since 2011

It has been the dream of Malayalees of Palm Beach, and neighboring counties, to have our own association. January 9, 2011 was a "GOLDEN DAY" in the fulfillment of that dream. A small group of people met and started the long awaited Kerala Association of Palm Beach.

The Malayalee Association of Palm Beach (KAPB) is one of the oldest and most inclusive Indian regional associations. As a not-for-profit organization, we are dedicated to individually and collectively promote and instill the culture and traditions of Kerala along with the unique heritage of its members.

Kerala Association of Palm Beach

Our Vision

The vision of the Malayalee Association of Palm Beach (KAPB) is to unite the Malayalee community to celebrate, preserve, and promote the rich traditions and cultural values of Kerala among friends, families, and younger generations. We aim to engage the Malayalee community in a wide range of activities encompassing cultural, educational, professional, social, sporting, welfare, and recreational areas.

Learn More →

Our Mission

KAPB Community Services is committed to empowering children, families, and individuals, fostering the growth of vibrant and thriving communities. We are dedicated to preserving and promoting Malayalee heritage and culture within our diverse multicultural society.

WHAT WE DO

Connecting Community, Celebrating Culture

At the Kerala Association of Palm Beach, we are dedicated to fostering a strong, vibrant community through various cultural, social, and educational initiatives:

  • Celebrate Kerala Festivals
  • Partner with Local Groups
  • Promote Heritage and Culture
  • Encourage Cultural Assimilation
  • Foster Goodwill and Harmony
